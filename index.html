The story behind this site

This site started as a doodle on lined paper. I'd been teaching myself to code, mostly Python, and kept getting stuck in tutorials I could follow but couldn't build anything from. So I picked something I actually care about, Nepali songs, and decided to build a home for their lyrics. One song page at a time, then a shared stylesheet, a dark purple theme, a 🎶 favicon, and finally this homepage, built from my own sketch with a search bar and a sliding row of song cards. I mix Devanagari and Romanized lyrics on purpose, because that's how I actually read these songs. Along the way I learned what divs, classes and ids really do, and I got help with the JavaScript and worked through what every line does. It's still growing, and every new song I add teaches me something.
